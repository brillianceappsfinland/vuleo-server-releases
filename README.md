# Vuleo Server

Vuleo Server brings the folders on your computer, your M3U playlists and your Xtream Codes accounts together, and serves them to the [Vuleo](https://vuleo.tv) app on your home network.

**Download for Windows:** [vuleo-server-setup.exe](https://github.com/brillianceappsfinland/vuleo-server-releases/releases/latest/download/vuleo-server-setup.exe) (Windows 10 or later, 64-bit)

Run the installer and open the page it shows at the end. What changed in each version is on the [releases page](https://github.com/brillianceappsfinland/vuleo-server-releases/releases).

More about the server: https://vuleo.tv/server.html
